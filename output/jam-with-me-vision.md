# JAM WITH ME

## The North Star

**Musicians shouldn't jam alone.**

Every city has thousands of guitarists who've never met a drummer. Bassists who practice in bedrooms. Singers with no band. The barrier isn't talent - it's discovery.

Jam With Me breaks that barrier.

---

## The Vision

**A world where any musician can find their people, anywhere, anytime.**

Open the app. See who's nearby. Say hi. Start jamming.

No auditions. No gatekeepers. No pretense.

Just people making music together.

---

## Core Beliefs

**All skill levels belong.** The beginner with 3 chords and the session pro with 30 years both deserve a jam partner.

**Good vibes only.** Music is vulnerable. The platform must feel safe, welcoming, supportive.

**Trust is earned.** Verification builds confidence. Reviews build reputation. The community polices itself.

**Low friction wins.** Every tap between "I want to jam" and "let's jam" is a dropout. Remove them all.

---

## The Experience

### Find Your People
Scroll a feed of nearby musicians. See who's looking to jam tonight vs. who's building a band.
Chat 1-1 and 1-many. 

### Connect Directly
No swipes. No matching algorithms. See someone interesting? Say hi. Start a conversation. Keep it simple.

### Host a Jam
Got a garage? A practice space? A park with good acoustics? Create a jam session. Set the vibe, the style.
Be able to apply a time and place, schedule / calendar of upcoming jams in the area, as well as improv.
Approve who joins.

### Build Your Reputation
After each jam, leave quick feedback. Vibe check. Reliability. Over time, your profile tells your story.

### Capture the Moment
Great jam? Record it. Upload it. Share it. Build a library of your musical journey.

## Success Looks Like

- A guitarist in Austin finds a drummer before their move-in boxes are unpacked
- A shy vocalist joins their first ever jam, leaves with 3 new friends
- A jazz trio that met on the app books their first gig
- A 50-year-old returning to music finds patience and encouragement
- A recording from a random Tuesday jam becomes someone's favorite track

---

## The Journey

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Foundation** | Profiles + Discovery - musicians find each other |
| **Connection** | Messaging + Jams - musicians connect and meet |
| **Trust** | Reviews + Verification - community builds confidence |
| **Memory** | Recordings - jams become shareable moments |
| **Scale** | Growth - one city becomes many |

---

## What We're NOT Building

- A dating app with instruments
- A hiring marketplace for session musicians
- A competitive ranking system
- A social media feed for followers/clout
- A music production or learning tool

---

## The Bet

**If we make it ridiculously easy to find compatible musicians nearby, people will jam more.**

More jams → more connections → more music → more joy.

That's it. That's the whole thing.
